Privacy notice

Version of 8 October 2026. Remiel is offered in the United States only.

Who we are. Remiel is provided by Remiel Technologies, a trade name of Beverly Index LLC, a United States company. Remiel is offered in the United States only. Write to privacy@remielengine.ai about anything in this notice.

In short. The record of the devices around you stays on your phone. Remiel’s server holds no name, email address or password for your account. What syncs between your own devices is encrypted on your device with a key the server does not hold. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising, and Remiel carries no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone. The identifiers, names and signal readings of devices near you; the times and your positions when they were heard; your trips, waypoints and notes; your labels, the people and rooms you list at home, the care plans you write for them (check-in times, medicine names and times, appointments and the order of calls) with the marks you make, the photographs you attach, and your Evidence log. Sightings older than 180 days are deleted. You can remove everything by clearing Remiel’s data or removing the app.

What our server holds. An account number and a number for each of your devices; your plan; the version of the terms you accepted, when you accepted it, and the country you signed up from; the content you sync, encrypted so that we cannot read it; and reports from sensors you connect, sealed by the sensor and kept for 7 days. If you buy a plan, it also holds the Stripe customer and subscription numbers, the plan and its status, and a record of your agreement to automatic renewal: the plan, the period, the price, the terms version, the country and the time. The law asks us to keep that record for three years.

Positions sent for a lookup. When you ask for a street name, a route or a place, Remiel sends the position, the points or the name to our server, and our server asks a public map service built on OpenStreetMap: Nominatim for streets and places, and the FOSSGIS service for routes. Our server sends nothing that identifies you. It keeps each street name for 30 days against the position rounded to about 11 m, with nothing about who asked. The identifiers of cell towers your phone uses are checked against the OpenCelliD register in the same way.

Services your phone reaches directly. Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, plate camera tiles from the DeFlock data service, and a copy of the security news feed from GitHub. Like any website, each of them receives your internet address.

Payments. You pay on a page that Stripe hosts. Stripe receives your card, your email address and your billing address, and handles them under its own privacy policy. We never receive your card number.

Service providers. Our server runs on Vercel, our database on Turso, and the web pages on GitHub Pages. They process data for us and keep their own records of requests, such as internet addresses and times, under their own policies. They may process data in the United States and in other countries.

Storage on your device. Remiel keeps its data in your browser or app storage, because it cannot work without it. It sets no tracking cookies.

Your choices. You can delete your account in Remiel: open Your account and devices, then Delete this account from the server. This removes everything our server holds for you, except the record of agreement to automatic renewal, which is kept for three years. You can ask us for a copy of what we hold, or for its deletion, at privacy@remielengine.ai, and we answer within 45 days. Residents of some states have further rights under the law of their state, and we honour them.

Requests from authorities. We cannot read the content you sync. We answer valid legal process only with what we hold, and we tell you about a request where the law allows.

Children. Remiel is for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security. Synced content is encrypted on your device, Evidence entries are hashed and chained, and connections to our server are encrypted. Report a security flaw to security@remielengine.ai.

Changes. When this notice changes, Remiel shows the new version. Version of 8 October 2026.
