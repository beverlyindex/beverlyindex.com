REMIEL: COVARA

How Covara uses health data

From a watch or ring, through Android Health Connect. Read only, and only when you allow it.

WHAT COVARA READS

Sleep
When you slept, and the stages (awake, light, deep, REM)
Heart rate
Readings your watch or ring saved
Breathing rate
Readings your watch or ring saved
How far back
The last three days, each time Covara sends them

WHAT IT IS FOR

For you
Your nights in Covara: Health, Sleep and breathing
For your team
Only the people you chose to share your care with in Covara, and only while you share with them
Alerts
None come from these readings; alerts come only from a Covara room sensor

WHAT COVARA NEVER DOES

Writing
Covara never writes or changes anything in Health Connect
Selling
Your health data is never sold, rented or used for advertising
Other uses
It is not used to train AI, and it is not a medical diagnosis

YOUR CHOICE, ANY TIME

Stop
Open Health Connect, App permissions, Remiel: Covara, and turn the permissions off
Delete
To have readings already sent removed, write to privacy@remielengine.ai
