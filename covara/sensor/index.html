REMIEL: COVARA

Room sensor setup

Put Covara on a sensor board, then pair it with a 6 digit code. No camera; it sees with a 60 GHz radar.

1 · PUT COVARA ON THE BOARD

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Safari cannot do this step).
  2. Plug the sensor board into the computer with a USB cable that carries data.
  3. Click the button below, choose the board in the list, and click Install. It takes about a minute.

This browser cannot install it. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

Boards: an ESP32 DevKit (radar on pins 16 and 17) or a DFRobot FireBeetle 2 ESP32-C6 (radar on D3 and D2), with the DFRobot C1001 60 GHz radar.

2 · PAIR IT WITH COVARA

  1. In Covara: Settings, Room sensors. Type the room name, choose Bedside or Ceiling, and tap Pair a Covara sensor. A 6 digit code appears.
  2. On a phone, open the Wi-Fi list and join Covara-Setup-…. A page opens (or go to 192.168.4.1).
  3. Enter the home Wi-Fi name and password, and the 6 digit code. Tap Connect.
  4. Within a minute the sensor appears in Covara under Room sensors, and its readings begin.

3 · WHERE IT GOES

  1. Bedside: about 1 metre above the head of the bed, facing the chest, within 1.5 metres. Sleep, breathing and heart rate.
  2. Ceiling: in the middle of the room, about 2.7 metres up, facing down. Falls, and where they are in the room.
  3. One sensor does one of these at a time. A bedroom and a bathroom need one each.
  4. To start over (new Wi-Fi or a new home), hold the board's BOOT button for 5 seconds.

PLEASE KNOW

Covara and its sensor help a family notice changes. They are not a medical device and do not diagnose anything. Heart rate in particular is a trend, not an exact reading. In an emergency, call 911.
