Who we are

DREAM CODE is a dream journal and dream analysis app, the companion to the book The Dream Code: Understanding Your Mind Through the Science of Dreams by Randolph R Beverly Jr. It is made by Empyrean Scientifica, a trade name of Beverly Index LLC, United States ("we", "us"). This policy covers the DREAM CODE app for Android, the DREAM CODE web console at beverly-index.com/dreamcode and the DREAM CODE server they use.

Questions or requests about your information: rrbjr@remielengine.ai.

In short

  • Your journal is yours. You can export it at any time, for free, and delete it at any time.
  • The private parts of your dreams are encrypted before they are stored on our server.
  • To write an analysis or a chat reply, the text needed for it is sent to our AI provider for that request only.
  • Paid plans are bought only in the Android app, through Google Play. We never receive your card or other payment details.
  • There are no ads in DREAM CODE. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • The app and the web console contain no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • DREAM CODE is for adults (18 or older) and is a wellness tool, not a medical service.

What we collect

Your account

When you create an account: your email address, your password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash, never in readable form), the display name you choose, your language, your confirmation that you are 18 or older, the time you accepted the Terms and this policy, and the date the account was created. We also keep your plan, how many analyses and chat messages you have used in the current week or month (to apply your plan's allowance) and, if you use a tester code, which code and when.

Your journal

If you are signed in, each dream you save is kept on our server so it is safe if you lose or change your phone: its title, text, the date you dreamt it, the dream types you choose, the recording details you fill in (such as sleep quality, emotional tone, people, the dominant emotion, how it ended, sensations and intensity), your reflections, its analysis and your chat with Dream AI. These fields are encrypted together with AES-256-GCM before they are stored. So that an overwrite from another device can be undone, earlier versions of the title, text and reflection are kept, encrypted, up to the last 20 versions of each dream.

Kept in readable form beside them: the dream's id, dates and times, a change number used for syncing, the main dream type of its analysis, the id of a community post you shared from it, and a keyed fingerprint (HMAC) of the analysed text, which only tells the server whether the text changed since it was analysed.

Without an account, your dreams stay on your device only and nothing about them reaches our server.

Dream Community (only if you use it)

Your community name (handle), the posts, comments and chat room messages you publish, your likes, the people you block, and the reports you file (the reason and an optional note). When you share a dream, you choose which parts go into the post (title, text, types, symbols); your analysis and reflections are never shared. Community posts, comments and room messages are visible to anyone who opens Dream Community, including people who are not signed in, with your handle and its initials. Your email and display name are never shown. Trends and the symbol map are worked out from visible posts and show no personal information.

Reports about AI content

If you report an analysis or a Dream AI reply, we keep an encrypted copy of the reported AI text (never your dream itself or anything else from your journal), the reason, your optional note, the time and the status of the report.

Moderation and safety

Community suspensions (their end date and reason) and a log of moderation decisions (which moderator took which action on which item, when, and an optional moderator note; the log does not hold the item's text).

Sign-in and technical information

  • A record for each signed-in session (an id and its time), so that you can stay signed in for up to 60 days and sign out. The sign-in tokens themselves stay on your device.
  • If you ask to reset your password: a hashed one-time token that works for one hour. The reset link is sent to your email address.
  • To protect accounts from guessing and abuse, the server limits repeated requests by network address or account. These counts are held in memory for a short time; where one is kept in the database, the network address is replaced by a keyed hash.
  • Our hosting provider tells the server which country a request comes from. It is used, without being stored, to decide whether new accounts can be opened there and to show the right crisis support lines.
  • Our hosting providers may keep short-lived technical logs (such as network address, time and the page or address requested) under their own policies. Our own server logs never contain dream text, reflections, chat messages, replies, passwords, tokens or email addresses.

On your device

The app keeps a copy of your journal and of changes waiting to sync in the device's browser storage (IndexedDB), together with your sign-in tokens, your settings and the last known account and plan details. The web console also keeps its own files in a cache so it can open offline. Voice input, if you use it, turns speech into text with your device's or browser's speech recognition service (for example Google's); DREAM CODE receives only the text you keep. Morning reminders in the Android app are scheduled on the device and send nothing to us.

Subscriptions and payments

The paid plans (Dreamer and Dream Code Plus) can be bought only in the DREAM CODE Android app, through Google Play Billing; they cannot be bought on the web console. You pay Google under Google Play's terms, and Google handles the payment as an independent party under its own privacy policy. We never receive your card or other payment details, and we do not keep your billing address or your Google account details.

When you buy, the app tells Google Play which DREAM CODE account the purchase is for, using an identifier of that account (a random code, not your email, your name or any other personal detail). The app then hands the purchase token that Google issues to our server. The server asks Google about the purchase, checks that it was made for your account, confirms (acknowledges) it to Google and opens your plan. Google can also notify our server when a subscription renews, changes, is cancelled or ends; the server then reads the subscription from Google again.

For each subscription we keep: the purchase token, the account it belongs to, the product and plan, the billing period (monthly or yearly), the subscription's state as Google reports it, when the current paid period ends, whether it renews automatically, whether it is a test purchase, which purchase replaced it after a change of plan, and when we last checked it. On your account we keep the plan, that it comes from Google Play, and when it ends. We use these records only to open and keep your plan, to stop a purchase from being used by another account, and to refuse purchases that Google does not confirm.

These records are kept while your account exists and are deleted with it. A Google Play subscription that still renews must be cancelled in Google Play before the account can be deleted, because deleting the account cannot stop Google from charging you; the app tells you so if you try. Google keeps its own records of your purchases under its own policies.

How we use your information

  • To check community content before it is published, to handle reports, to block, and to moderate.

We do not use your journal for advertising, we do not sell or rent your information, we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers, and we do not send marketing emails. The only email DREAM CODE sends is the password reset link you ask for.

Sensitive information. Dreams can touch health, sexuality, beliefs or relationships. You decide what you write. Everything in your journal is treated as private, is encrypted at rest, and is used only to provide DREAM CODE to you. Dreams of the Sexual/Intimate type can be journaled but cannot be shared in the community.

Artificial intelligence

Dream analyses and Dream AI chat replies are written by an AI model, currently Claude by Anthropic, from what you write. They are reflections for your wellbeing, not medical, psychological or other professional advice, and not a diagnosis. They may be wrong. You can report any analysis or reply from inside the app.

For an analysis we send the dream's text, title, date, the types you chose and your recording details, together with a short list of symbols that recur in your recent analysed dreams. For a chat reply we send the dream's text, title and date, a summary of its analysis, the recent turns of the conversation and your message. Your reflections are not sent. Before something is published in the community, its text is also checked by the AI provider. Each request carries only what it needs.

Anthropic states that, by default, it does not use inputs or outputs of its commercial API to train its models, and that it deletes them within 30 days, except where longer retention applies under its usage policies or the law.

These providers may process information in the United States and in other countries where they operate. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of a person when we believe in good faith that this is necessary. If Beverly Index LLC is ever part of a merger or sale, your information would move only under the protections of this policy, and we would tell you first.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The private fields of your dreams, their earlier versions and copies of reported AI text are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they reach the database. Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes. The encryption is not end to end: the server holds the key, because it must read a dream to send it for analysis, and because a lost phone should never cost you your journal. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

How long we keep it

  • Your journal: while your account exists. A dream you delete stays in Recently deleted for 30 days, where you can restore it; after that its text, analysis, chat and earlier versions are erased and only its id and deletion date remain, so other devices know not to bring it back. Those remain until you delete your account.
  • Sessions: up to 60 days, or until you sign out. Usage counts and password reset records: until you delete your account.
  • Subscription records: while your account exists; they are deleted with it.
  • Community content: until you delete it or your account. A post or comment you delete is hidden from everyone at once; its text stays in our database until your account is deleted, so reports about it can still be reviewed.
  • Reports about AI content: until a moderator resolves them and then 90 days, or until you delete your account, whichever comes first.
  • Moderation log: kept to keep the community safe, also after an account is deleted. It holds no item text.
  • After you delete your account: we keep only a minimal record that the account existed (its internal id, the dates it was created and deleted, its language and the time the Terms were accepted), with no email, name, handle, password or content, so that no old sign-in can work again.
  • Backups: our database provider may keep backups for a limited time, so deleted information may remain in a backup until it expires.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to ask what information we hold about you, to receive a copy, to have it corrected or erased, to object to or restrict some uses, or to complain to your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). Che cosa raccogliamo

Il tuo diario

Se hai effettuato l'accesso, ogni sogno che salvi viene conservato sul nostro server, così resta al sicuro se perdi o cambi telefono: titolo, testo, data in cui l'hai sognato, i tipi di sogno che scegli, i dettagli che compili (come qualità del sonno, tono emotivo, persone, emozione dominante, come è finito, sensazioni e intensità), le tue riflessioni, la sua analisi e la tua chat con Dream AI. Restano leggibili accanto a questi: l'identificativo del sogno, date e orari, un numero di modifica usato per la sincronizzazione, il tipo di sogno principale della sua analisi, l'identificativo di un post della community condiviso a partire da esso, e un'impronta con chiave (HMAC) del testo analizzato, che serve solo a capire se il testo è cambiato dopo l'analisi.

Le sospensioni dalla community (data di fine e motivo) e un registro delle decisioni di moderazione (quale moderatore ha preso quale azione su quale elemento, quando, e una nota facoltativa del moderatore; il registro non contiene il testo dell'elemento).

Accesso e informazioni tecniche

Intelligenza artificiale

Le analisi dei sogni e le risposte di Dream AI in chat sono scritte da un modello di intelligenza artificiale, attualmente Claude di Anthropic, a partire da ciò che scrivi. Sono riflessioni per il tuo benessere, non consigli medici, psicologici o professionali, e non sono una diagnosi. Possono essere sbagliate. Puoi segnalare qualsiasi analisi o risposta direttamente dall'app.

Per un'analisi inviamo il testo del sogno, il titolo, la data, i tipi che hai scelto e i dettagli che hai compilato, insieme a un breve elenco dei simboli che ricorrono nei tuoi sogni analizzati di recente. Per una risposta in chat inviamo il testo, il titolo e la data del sogno, un riassunto della sua analisi, gli ultimi scambi della conversazione e il tuo messaggio. Le tue riflessioni non vengono inviate. Prima che qualcosa venga pubblicato nella community, anche il suo testo viene controllato dal fornitore di AI. Ogni richiesta contiene solo ciò che le serve.

Anthropic dichiara che, per impostazione predefinita, non usa gli input e gli output della sua API commerciale per addestrare i propri modelli e che li cancella entro 30 giorni, salvo i casi in cui una conservazione più lunga sia prevista dalle sue regole d'uso o dalla legge.

Sicurezza

I dati viaggiano su connessioni cifrate (HTTPS). I campi privati dei tuoi sogni, le loro versioni precedenti e le copie dei testi dell'AI segnalati vengono cifrati con AES-256-GCM prima di arrivare al database. Le password sono conservate solo come hash scrypt. La cifratura non è end to end: la chiave è sul server, perché il server deve leggere un sogno per inviarlo all'analisi e perché perdere il telefono non deve mai costarti il diario. Nessun metodo di conservazione o trasmissione è perfettamente sicuro, ma lavoriamo per proteggere i tuoi dati.

Per quanto tempo li conserviamo

Un sogno che cancelli resta in Eliminati di recente per 30 giorni, dove puoi ripristinarlo; dopo, il testo, l'analisi, la chat e le versioni precedenti vengono cancellati e restano solo il suo identificativo e la data di cancellazione, perché gli altri dispositivi sappiano di non riportarlo indietro. Questi restano finché non elimini l'account.
  • Sessioni: fino a 60 giorni, o finché non esci. Conteggi d'uso e richieste di reimpostazione della password: finché non elimini l'account.
  • Dati degli abbonamenti: finché esiste il tuo account; vengono cancellati insieme a esso.
  • Contenuti della community: finché non li cancelli tu o non elimini l'account. Un post o un commento che cancelli viene nascosto subito a tutti; il suo testo resta nel nostro database finché non elimini l'account, così le segnalazioni che lo riguardano possono ancora essere esaminate.
