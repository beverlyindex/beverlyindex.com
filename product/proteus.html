PROTEUS: Cognitive ISR

Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance through cognitive signature analysis. Non-cooperative profiling from text and voice.

Defense Application

Manage the signature.

PROTEUS is the defense application of Neural Oscillation Signature Theory. Where NOUS reads a cognitive signature to infer clinical state, PROTEUS manages a signature to assess what an adversary reveals, conceals, or fabricates in their communications.

The platform processes intercepted text, open-source intelligence, debriefings, and voice communications through the same NOST analytical pipeline, producing cognitive profiles without subject cooperation, hardware, or physiological access.

Core capabilities

  • Non-cooperative cognitive profiling from natural language
  • Cognitive deception and influence detection in adversarial communications
  • Longitudinal cognitive tracking of high-value individuals
  • Real-time analysis of intercepted voice and text streams
  • Integration with existing ISR data pipelines

PROTEUS is distinct from REMIEL Engine (the Level 3 reader component inside NOUS) and from Remiel Technologies (the licensed builder entity).
